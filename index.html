About

Vyom Kulshrestha

Founder, Heliox OS • Systems & AI Engineer

I build open-source systems that let AI act on computers under explicit safety, permission, and verification boundaries.

My work spans Heliox OS, a local-first multimodal desktop agent; FerrumOS, a bootable Rust research operating system; and Nucle-OS with NucleScript, a software-defined DNA-storage stack and domain-specific language.

I work across Rust kernels and no_std userland, Python agent runtimes, TypeScript interfaces, model evaluation, CI, releases, and product design.

I am a Computer Science student at VIT, currently interning at FinAdvantage, and open to research collaborations, engineering roles, open-source contributors, and project sponsors.

Research

Published August 7, 2026 • Zenodo

When Agents Control the Kernel

A JEPA World Model Safety Gate with Empirical False-Negative Decomposition

Independent technical report by Vyom Kulshrestha

The report presents FerrumOS's provider-independent OS-level action-mediation architecture, combining deterministic enforcement with action-conditioned JEPA transition screening before capability-gated syscalls.

13,697OS-state transitions
3,639QEMU episodes
500Counterfactual episodes
81.4%Rules + JEPA balanced accuracy

What the evidence says

  • Rules alone reached 75.2% balanced accuracy; rules plus JEPA reached 81.4%.
  • A strong per-action mean baseline reached 81.2%, so the report does not claim JEPA is necessary for the binary decision.
  • Measured mean preview latency was 1.35 ms at H=1 and 1.59 ms at H=5 in the published study.
  • Remaining failures separate into missing semantic assets, long-horizon process accumulation, and heap underprediction.
Evidence boundary

This is an authored systems-research evaluation, not an independent replication, formal safety proof, physical deployment result, or certification.
